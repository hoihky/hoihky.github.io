About

I am a software architect with a career spanning capital markets, enterprise platforms, and open-source systems programming. Today I am especially passionate about game engine development and the C# / .NET ecosystem — but that work builds on years of shipping production software where latency, reliability, and correctness mattered every trading day.

Capital markets

I have worked at a top-tier investment bank and a proprietary trading firm, building and operating systems at the core of options and equity trading. That experience covered order routing, pricing, risk, and the low-latency infrastructure that keeps desks competitive — work where milliseconds and correctness are both non-negotiable.

Enterprise software

Beyond finance, I have delivered large-scale business systems including a document management platform, a call center solution, and Office productivity plugins. These projects demanded robust workflows, integration with existing enterprise tools, and UIs that non-technical users could rely on daily.

Current focus

I now channel that systems background into open-source work: a C++ game engine, .NET libraries, an embedded OLAP database, and technical books that teach by building real software — from Vulkan rendering and Roslyn source generators to cross-platform UI and design patterns in production codebases.

Across domains, I gravitate toward problems where performance, tooling, and developer experience intersect — whether that means a trading stack, an enterprise workflow, or a game engine pipeline.

A modular browser 3D game engine with TypeScript, Three.js, and ECS architecture — game logic in components and systems, rendering behind a WebGL2/WebGPU backend abstraction. Includes the Nova Collector sample game; evolving toward commercial-grade web tooling.
